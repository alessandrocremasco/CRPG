# CRPG

RPG game developed in C.

*"The princess of Gondor was kidnapped.\nWith a sword and a shield you were assigned the mission to bring back to kingdom its beloved princess.\nVenture into the deep, dark dungeon. Move across the caves and slash every creature that comes your way to find the princess and succeding the quest!"*

*Good luck, slayer!*
